# GPU Quick Reference

## NVIDIA H100
- Column: Data Center
- Description: Hopper data center GPU optimized for large-scale AI training and inference.
- Typical use: LLM training, high-throughput inference, HPC clusters.

## NVIDIA H200
- Column: Data Center
- Description: Hopper-class accelerator with larger HBM memory for demanding LLM workloads.
- Typical use: Memory-intensive inference and training.

## NVIDIA A100
- Column: Data Center
- Description: Ampere data center GPU widely used for AI training, inference, and HPC.
- Typical use: Established production training/inference fleets.

## NVIDIA L40S
- Column: Inference
- Description: Ada-based GPU for inference, graphics, and video-heavy AI workloads.
- Typical use: Multi-modal inference and media-centric AI pipelines.

## NVIDIA RTX 4090
- Column: Workstation
- Description: High-end Ada consumer GPU often used for workstation prototyping and local inference.
- Typical use: Local experiments, prototyping, development workstations.

## NVIDIA RTX 6000 Ada
- Column: Workstation
- Description: Professional workstation GPU for enterprise visualization and AI development.
- Typical use: Enterprise workstation rendering + AI development.

## AMD Instinct MI300X
- Column: Data Center
- Description: AMD data center accelerator for large model training and inference workloads.
- Typical use: Large-model acceleration in AMD-centric stacks.
